Frequently asked questions

Answers for the Nexauren foundation and future platform.

Are the tools free?

The platform is being designed around free, accessible utilities. Individual tools may have different limits or requirements in the future.

How are tools added?

A tool is registered in frontend/data/tools.json. The catalog and categories read that registry automatically.

Will I need an account?

The foundation does not require an account. Account features can be added later without changing the public catalog architecture.

Can I use Nexauren on a phone?

Yes. The interface is being built responsively for mobile, tablet and desktop screens.
