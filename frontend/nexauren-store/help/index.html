FAQ & Help

How do purchases work?

Marketplace products are one-time digital purchases. Payment is verified by Nexauren before access is granted.

Where are my products?

Purchased products are connected to your Nexauren account and available from My Library when an entitlement is active.

Do I need a separate Store account?

No. Marketplace uses the same Nexauren account and session.
