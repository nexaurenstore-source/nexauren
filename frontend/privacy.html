Privacy Policy

A foundation policy for the Nexauren platform.

Information we collect

Nexauren should collect only information needed to provide a requested feature, operate the service, prevent abuse and understand basic service performance.

Tool processing

Where a tool can process content entirely in the browser, the platform should avoid sending that content to a server unnecessarily. Server-side processing will be explained on the relevant tool page when introduced.

Usage and history

Future account-based history and usage records will be stored only when the feature requires them and will be handled according to the final published policy.

Security

Security controls, HTTPS, access control and protected sessions will be implemented before account features are enabled.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be directed through the contact method published by Nexauren when the public contact channel is enabled.
